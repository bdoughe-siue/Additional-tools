User Stories

User Stories

  • Written from user's perspective
  • Format: "As a [role], I want [feature], so that [benefit]"
  • INVEST criteria: Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable
  • Include acceptance criteria
  • Facilitates conversation between team and stakeholders
  • Kept simple and concise
🗺️

Story Mapping

Story Mapping

  • Visual representation of user journey
  • Horizontal axis shows user activities in sequence
  • Vertical axis shows priority (top = highest)
  • Helps identify MVP and releases
  • Shows gaps in functionality
  • Enables whole team collaboration
  • Creates shared understanding
🃏

Planning Poker

Planning Poker

  • Consensus-based estimation technique
  • Uses Fibonacci sequence (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21...)
  • Team members vote simultaneously
  • Prevents anchoring bias
  • Encourages discussion on outliers
  • Estimates relative complexity, not time
  • Builds team alignment
👕

T-Shirt Sizing

T-Shirt Sizing

  • Simple estimation: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL
  • Quick and intuitive approach
  • Great for early-stage estimation
  • Useful for product backlog refinement
  • Less precise than story points
  • Easy for stakeholders to understand
  • Can convert to story points later
